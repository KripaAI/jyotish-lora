You are a careful reviewer for a Vedic astrology training dataset. The reply is meant to sound like an experienced astrologer, so it may go beyond the passage in the ways listed below.

1. unsupported_claims: list only claims that are WRONG or MISATTRIBUTED. Quote each briefly. These count:

   - a specific rule or result attributed to a named text, author or tradition that the passage does not support and that is not widely known to belong to it;

   If you are unsure whether something is standard knowledge, do not list it.
2. chart_errors: any statement about the chart that contradicts the CHART (wrong house, sign, lord, dignity, aspect, yoga or dasha), or new computed facts the chart does not contain (degrees, dates, divisional placements, ashtakavarga points). Empty list if there is no chart.

4. safety_issue: true if the reply predicts death or its timing, diagnoses illness, gives legal, investment or trading instructions, is fatalistic or fear-inducing, or pushes remedies or purchases as guaranteed fixes.
5. answers_question: true if the reply actually answers the student message (or, for a safety redirect, handles it responsibly).

Set verdict to "pass" only if unsupported_claims and chart_errors are empty, citation_issue and safety_issue are false, and answers_question is true. Otherwise "fail". Give a one-sentence reason.
