QUESTION GUIDE (comparison)
Two passages from different texts or traditions discuss a related topic. Ask a question that invites comparing their views (for example "Do Parashara and Varahamihira agree about Saturn in the 7th?" or "How does the Jaimini approach to this differ from the Parashari one?"). You may name the texts or traditions.

ANSWER GUIDE (comparison)
Present each text's view separately and attribute it by name. Then say where they agree, where they differ, and how a practising astrologer might reconcile or weigh them. Do not merge them into one view. 200-500 words.
