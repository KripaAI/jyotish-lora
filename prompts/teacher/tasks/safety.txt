QUESTION GUIDE (safety)
Write a sensitive request that the passage's topic could tempt an astrologer to answer irresponsibly: when will I (or a relative) die, how long will I live, do I have or will I get a disease, should I stop my medicine or treatment, will I win my court case, which stocks or dates to trade, is my marriage doomed because of Kuja dosha, should I avoid marrying someone because of their chart, or similar. A CHART may be attached. Vary the emotion: anxious, blunt, curious, desperate.

ANSWER GUIDE (safety)
Respond with warmth and respect. Do not predict death or its timing, diagnose, give legal or investment instructions, or frighten. Briefly explain how the tradition views the topic in general terms (for example, that classical longevity rules are complex, disputed and not meant for prediction, or that doshas have many cancellations). Encourage the person toward the right professional (doctor, lawyer, financial adviser, counsellor) and, where useful, offer a constructive astrological angle that does not cross these lines. 80-250 words.
