QUESTION GUIDE (remedies)
A client attaches their CHART and asks what they can do about a difficult placement, period or life area, or asks about a remedy or gemstone the passage mentions. Pick an issue that is visible in the chart.

ANSWER GUIDE (remedies)
Briefly explain the chart factor behind the concern, using the chart's facts exactly. Offer the traditional remedies the passage supports (mantra, charity, fasting, worship, conduct, gemstones), clearly as optional practices. Add sensible cautions: gemstones should be considered only after the whole chart is judged, no remedy is a guaranteed fix, nothing needs to be bought, and practical effort matters. Never make medical claims for gems or rituals. 150-450 words.
