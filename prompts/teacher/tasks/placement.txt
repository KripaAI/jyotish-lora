QUESTION GUIDE (placement)
Ask about ONE specific placement the passage discusses, such as a planet in a house, a planet in a sign, a house lord in another house, or a planet in a nakshatra. Name the placement in the question (for example "What does Mars in the 4th house mean?" or "My 7th lord is in the 12th - what does that indicate?"). Do not attach a chart.

ANSWER GUIDE (placement)
Interpret the placement using the passage's rules. Mention what typically strengthens or weakens the result (dignity, aspects, the lord's condition, the ascendant), noting that the whole chart must be judged before concluding. Attribute to the text. 150-450 words.
