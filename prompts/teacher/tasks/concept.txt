QUESTION GUIDE (concept)
Ask about a concept, rule, definition or principle that the passage explains: what a yoga is and when it works, what a house or planet signifies, how a technique is applied, what a term means. Mix beginner and advanced students. One message may ask "why" a rule holds.

ANSWER GUIDE (concept)
Explain the concept clearly and accurately, as a teacher would. State the rule, the conditions under which it applies, and any qualifications the passage gives. Attribute it to the text or tradition. Give a short practical illustration in words (no invented chart). 150-450 words.
